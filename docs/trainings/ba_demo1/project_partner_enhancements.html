Partner Management Enhancements

Branch: demo/partner-management-enhancements  |  Source: April 2026 brainstorming session

Features selected for development to enhance partner self-service and organizer efficiency.

Selected Features

#1

RSVP Visibility in Portal

Partners can see their own RSVP status per meeting in the portal. Backend data already exists — frontend only.

#2

Post-Meeting Notes Sharing

Organizer can choose to "publish" meeting notes to the partner via a toggle on the existing notes model.

#3

Meeting Minutes & Action Items

Structured action items section in meeting notes. Partners receive summary after meeting.

#4

Documents & Attachments Sharing

Organizers upload files to partner profile (invoices, contracts, logos). Partners can download.

Analytics clarification: BATbern already has a top-level analytics page visible to both organizers and partners covering attendance trends, YoY indicators, benchmarking, etc. — no new analytics features needed.
