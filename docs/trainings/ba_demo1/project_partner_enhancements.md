Nissim selected these partner management enhancements for development in the `demo/partner-management-enhancements` branch:

1. **RSVP Visibility in Portal** — Partners can see their own RSVP status per meeting in the portal (backend data already exists, frontend only)
2. **Post-Meeting Notes Sharing** — Organizer can choose to "publish" meeting notes to the partner (toggle on existing notes model)
3. **Meeting Minutes & Action Items** — Structured action items section in meeting notes; partners receive summary after meeting
4. **Documents & Attachments Sharing** — Organizers upload files to partner profile (invoices, contracts, logos); partners can download

**Analytics clarification:**
- BATbern already has a top-level analytics page visible to both organizers and partners covering attendance trends, YoY indicators, benchmarking, etc. — no new analytics features needed.

**Upcoming brainstorm topic:**
- Yearly invoice management for partner billing

**Why:** These were identified during an April 2026 brainstorming session on the demo branch to enhance partner self-service and organizer efficiency.
