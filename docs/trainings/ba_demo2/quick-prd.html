PRD — Partner Management Enhancements

Author: Nissim  |  Date: 2026-04-04  |  Complete

Executive Summary

BATbern Partner Management Enhancements extend the existing platform with two capability layers that eliminate the remaining analog gaps in the partner relationship lifecycle. Target users: BATbern organizers (managing 5–10 active sponsoring companies) and partner contacts.

Two problems solved:

  1. Invoice lifecycle is entirely manual — organizers create invoices by hand, track payment without tooling, and have no structured view of receivables
  2. Partner communication artifacts (meeting notes, action items) exist only in email, leaving partners without self-service access

BATbern becomes the single authoritative system for the full partner lifecycle — from first meeting invite through invoice settlement. The differentiator: partners open their portal and see meeting notes, action items, and invoices in one place; organizers see a real-time financial status board with zero manual tracking.

User Journeys

Journey 1: Organizer — Invoice Season (Happy Path)

Lukas, BATbern organizer. Mid-January. Last year: afternoon hunting billing addresses in email, hand-crafting PDFs in InDesign.

Opens Partners → Fees & Invoices. Fee table shows all 7 active partners. Clicks "Create All Drafts for 2026". Seven DRAFT invoices appear. Updates two fees inline. Selects all, clicks Send Invoices. Seven branded PDFs land in each billing contact inbox.

Three weeks later: five PAID, one SENT, one OVERDUE. Clicks overdue partner, sends reminder. Following week: all seven PAID.

Journey 2: Organizer — After Partner Meeting (Happy Path)

Day after the spring partner meeting. Lukas has his notes.

Opens meeting detail, pastes minutes, adds structured Action Items (three items with owner + due date), clicks Publish to Partner. Uploads signed sponsorship contract via Attachments tab.

Journey 3: Partner — First Login After Meeting (Happy Path)

Sandra, head of community at Acme AG. Needs to brief her CMO.

Opens Meetings tab — green "Notes Published" indicator. Opens: full minutes, action items. Downloads PDF. Opens Billing Info — enters address and PO number. Two days later, invoice arrives pre-addressed.

Journey 4: Partner — Invoice Confusion (Edge Case)

Sandra's accounts payable paid with wrong reference.

Opens portal: invoice status SENT. Emails Lukas. Lukas clicks Mark as Paid, enters date and bank reference. Invoice flips to PAID. Sandra refreshes — green.

Journey 5: Organizer — New Partner Onboarding (Admin Path)

New GOLD-tier sponsor joins mid-year.

Creates partner record, sets pro-rated fee, creates one DRAFT invoice manually. Partner fills billing info; invoice sent within the week. Separately: association moves offices — updates address in Admin → Invoice Settings.
