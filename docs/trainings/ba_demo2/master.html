Example 1: Quick Flow — Partner Enhancements

Single session, single agent (Mary) — brainstorm to PRD in one sitting. Best for small, focused feature sets.

Single Session
⚡
Brainstorm → PRD in One Session
Agent: Mary (Analyst)  |  Phases 1+2 combined
Activate the Analyst Agent, explore partner management pain points, brainstorm enhancements (invoice management, meeting notes sharing, RSVP visibility), then use the PM Agent to produce a complete PRD — all in a single sitting. Shows how BMAD adapts to smaller, well-understood features.
Feature: Partner Mgmt Enhancements FRs: 30 Scope: Invoice + Notes + RSVP
Artifact
📝
PRD: Partner Management Enhancements
Invoice lifecycle, meeting notes sharing, billing self-service
The PRD produced by the quick flow: invoice entity with DRAFT→SENT→PAID→OVERDUE lifecycle, PDF generation, partner billing self-service, meeting notes & action items publishing, RSVP visibility, attachment sharing. B2B SaaS patterns.
Type: SaaS B2B Entities: PartnerInvoice, BillingInfo

Example 2: Full Path — Active Meeting Agenda

Multi-agent, multi-session — 3 agents across Phases 1-3. Best for large, novel feature verticals.

1 Phase 1: Analysis

Complete Session

Brainstorming Session
Agent: Mary (Analyst)  |  Workflow: Brainstorm Project
Verbatim conversation + training annotations. Discover 35 feature ideas across 6 agenda items using Morphological Analysis, Role Playing, and Cross-Pollination. Includes the key breakthrough: meeting minutes as living document.
Ideas: 35 Themes: 6 Techniques: 3

2 Phase 2: Planning

Complete Session
🎨
UX Design Session
Agent: Sally (UX Designer)  |  Workflow: Create UX Design
Verbatim conversation + training annotations. Design the user experience: 5 user journeys, presenter mode layout, 12 custom components, projector-optimized dark theme. Includes 6 design directions and the composite A+E+F selection.
Journeys: 5 Components: 12 Steps: 14

📝
PRD Creation Session
Agent: John (PM)  |  Workflow: Create PRD
Verbatim conversation + training annotations. Synthesize brainstorm + UX spec into a full PRD: 35 FRs, 17 success criteria, 6 user journeys, scope & phasing, NFRs.
FRs: 35 Sections: 12 Journeys: 6

3 Phase 3: Solutioning

Complete Session
🏗
Architecture Session
Agent: Winston (Architect)  |  Workflow: Create Architecture

Entities: 11 Patterns: 30+ Steps: 8

Epics & Stories Session
