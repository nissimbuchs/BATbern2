Product Requirements Document
BATbern Active Partner Meeting Agenda

Author: Nissim  |  Date: 2026-04-04  |  Status: Complete  |  Classification: Brownfield Web App, Medium Complexity

Executive Summary

BATbern's bi-annual partner meetings currently run on verbal agreements, emailed PDFs, handwritten notes, and "someone takes minutes." Six agenda items — greeting, analytics review, audit sign-off, budget approval, topic discussion, and open topics — exist only as text in an ICS calendar invite. There is no governance trail for financial sign-offs, no structured record of decisions, and absent partners are completely excluded from the process.

This PRD defines the Active Partner Meeting Agenda — a system that transforms those 6 static agenda items into interactive, system-driven features within the existing BATbern platform. The organizer walks through the meeting in a Presenter Mode, and every action they take (toggling attendance, uploading a document, triggering a vote, typing a note) silently contributes to auto-assembling meeting minutes. The meeting documents itself as a byproduct of use.

The feature extends the meeting beyond the room: absent partners receive published minutes, participate in async fee acceptance, and provide feedback — ensuring no partner is ever out of the loop.

Target users: Organizer (meeting driver, sole input device), Present Partners (view projected screen, participate verbally), Absent Partners (async via portal), New Partners (first-year onboarding), Stand-Ins (non-system delegates represented by organizer).

Platform: Brownfield extension of BATbern (React 19 + Spring Boot microservices on AWS). The partner coordination service (Epic 8) and partner portal already exist.

What Makes This Special

1. Minutes as byproduct — The organizer never "creates minutes." Every interaction auto-populates a structured meeting record. After the meeting, the organizer reviews a near-complete document they barely need to edit.

2. Proxied group voting — Partners don't use devices during the meeting. The organizer casts approve/decline votes on behalf of each present partner, like a teller in parliament.

3. Configurable agenda engine — Not a hardcoded sequence. The organizer toggles which active agenda item types are relevant for each meeting. Custom passive items can be added.

4. Meeting extends beyond the room — Async fee acceptance, feedback pulses, and published minutes ensure absent partners have full context.

5. Action item carry-forward — Action items carry forward to the next meeting automatically. Items carried 2+ meetings get urgency indicators.

User Journeys

Journey 1: Organizer — Create & Prepare Meeting (Pre-Meeting)

Sarah, BATbern organizer, creates a new partner meeting. She selects which active agenda items are relevant, adds a custom passive item ("Venue proposal for 2027"), drags items to reorder, and configures the auditor email. The system sends ICS invites with deep-links. She monitors the Meeting Prep Dashboard — a checklist shows what's ready (audit PDF, budget doc, RSVP count, topic voting, open action items). When all critical items are green, "Start Presenter Mode" enables.

Journey 2: Organizer — Run Meeting in Presenter Mode (Live)

Sarah clicks "Start Presenter Mode." Dark theme activates. She walks through each item: Who Is Who gallery (toggle presence), Analytics (capture snapshot), Audit (PDF viewer + per-partner vote), Budget (vote + fee decision), Topics (discussion notes), Hot Topics (free-form). At every step: note panel visible, sidebar shows progress, auto-save confirms. After the last item, "End Meeting" transitions to Minutes Review.

Journey 3: Organizer — Review & Publish Minutes (Post-Meeting)

Auto-assembled minutes appear: attendance, snapshots, documents, votes, notes, action items. Sarah edits sections, adds a clarification, clicks "Publish & Send." All partners receive email with portal link. Fee acceptance and feedback pulse trigger automatically.

Journey 4: Absent Partner — Catch Up & Act (Async)

Marco couldn't attend. He receives the minutes email, reads everything on his phone. Fee acceptance request appears: CHF 2,400 (up from CHF 2,200). He clicks "Accept." 24 hours later, he fills in the feedback pulse. Fully informed, fully participating — despite never being in the room.

Journey 5: New Partner — First Meeting

Lisa logs in for the first time. Guided onboarding tour, context package. At the meeting, her company has a purple "New" badge. Veterans welcome her.

Journey 6: Next Meeting — Action Item Carry-Forward

Sarah creates the autumn meeting. 3 open action items from spring surface in the prep dashboard. During the meeting: one done, one extended, one carried forward. Items carried 2+ meetings get urgency indicators.

Scope & Phasing

Phase 1 — MVP

Layer 1: Foundation — PartnerMeeting entity, Agenda Builder, RSVP dashboard, Document viewer, Vote component
Layer 2: Meeting Flow — Presenter Mode, Who Is Who gallery, Note panel, Minutes auto-assembly, Linear navigation
Layer 3: Agenda Interactions — Attendance→minutes, Analytics snapshot, Audit+vote, Budget+vote, Prep checklist, Action items
Layer 4: Post-Meeting — Minutes review & publish, Fee acceptance, Fee tracker, Carry-forward, Stand-in delegation

Phase 2 — Quick Wins

ICS deep-links, auditor email config, partner profile cards, feedback pulse survey.

Phase 3 — Nice-to-Have

New partner onboarding tour, context package, historized analytics archive, minutes PDF export.

Out of Scope

Multi-device partner interaction, WebSocket features, video conferencing, partner self-scheduling, AI-generated summaries.

Functional Requirements

FR1: Meeting Management (7 requirements)

FR1.1 Create partner meetings with date, time, and location.

FR1.2 Select which active agenda item types are included (Greeting, Analytics, Audit, Budget, Topics, Hot Topics).

FR1.3 Add custom passive agenda items (title + description).

FR1.4 Reorder agenda items via drag-and-drop.

FR1.5 Send ICS calendar invites to all partners.

FR1.6 Maintain meeting status lifecycle: DRAFT → SCHEDULED → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED.

FR1.7 Configure auditor email; conditional ICS invite when audit item is on agenda.

FR2: RSVP & Attendance (5 requirements)

FR2.1 Partners confirm or decline meeting attendance via portal.

FR2.2 Display RSVP dashboard (confirmed / declined / pending).

FR2.3 Toggle partner presence during meeting via Who Is Who gallery.

FR2.4 Record stand-in delegates (free-text name, delegated by partner).

FR2.5 Display "New" badge for first-year partner companies.

FR3: Presenter Mode (8 requirements)

FR3.1 Full-screen dark theme optimized for projector (min 1280px).

FR3.2 Agenda sidebar with upcoming/active/completed/skipped states.

FR3.3 Linear navigation via Next/Previous and keyboard shortcuts (→/←/1-9).

FR3.4 Jump to any item via sidebar (non-destructive).

FR3.5 Persistent note panel at bottom with auto-save.

FR3.6 Auto-save all actions within 2 seconds.

FR3.7 Display auto-save indicator ("Saved ✓").

FR3.8 Keyboard: N (notes), V (vote), Esc (exit with confirmation).

FR4: Document Upload & Viewing (4 requirements)

FR4.1 Upload PDF and spreadsheet documents to agenda items.

FR4.2 Embedded document viewer within Presenter Mode.

FR4.3 Permanently attach documents to meeting record.

FR4.4 Support up to 20MB via S3 presigned URL.

FR5: Voting (7 requirements)

FR5.1 Start vote on votable agenda items.

FR5.2 Vote panel listing all present partners with approve/decline.

FR5.3 Cast votes on behalf of each present partner.

FR5.4 Cast votes for stand-in delegates with attribution.

FR5.5 Allow vote changes before completion.

FR5.6 Display vote result banner ("Approved — 8/8").

FR5.7 Record per-partner attribution in minutes.

FR6: Meeting Minutes (8 requirements)

FR6.1 Create PartnerMeetingMinutes in DRAFT when Presenter Mode starts.

FR6.2 Auto-populate attendance from Who Is Who data.

FR6.3 Auto-populate with votes, documents, snapshots, notes per item.

FR6.4 Review and edit auto-assembled minutes.

FR6.5 Add, edit, remove action items during review.

FR6.6 Publish minutes (DRAFT → PUBLISHED).

FR6.7 Email notifications with portal link to all partners.

FR6.8 Mobile-friendly minutes in partner portal.

FR7: Meeting Prep (3 requirements)

FR7.1 Prep checklist with readiness status per enabled item.

FR7.2 Green/yellow/red status with actionable links.

FR7.3 Soft-block Start when critical items missing.

FR8: Post-Meeting Workflows (6 requirements)

FR8.1 Trigger async fee acceptance after budget vote with fee decision.

FR8.2 Partners accept/decline fee via portal with optional comment.

FR8.3 Fee acceptance tracker (accepted / pending / declined per partner).

FR8.4 Fee reminders at 7d and 14d, then notify organizer.

FR8.5 Feedback pulse survey (3 questions) 24h after publication.

FR8.6 Aggregate feedback with historical trends.

FR9: Action Items (5 requirements)

FR9.1 Create action items at any agenda item (title, owner, deadline).

FR9.2 Include action items in minutes.

FR9.3 Surface open items from previous meetings when creating new meeting.

FR9.4 Mark done, extend deadline, or carry forward during review.

FR9.5 Urgency indicator on items carried 2+ meetings.

FR10: Analytics Snapshot (3 requirements)

FR10.1 Embed analytics dashboard in Analytics Review agenda item.

FR10.2 Capture immutable snapshot attached to minutes.

FR10.3 Store snapshots for year-over-year browsing.

Constraints & Dependencies

Platform Dependencies

  • Partner Coordination Service (Epic 8) — partner entities, roles, portal auth
  • Company/User Management Service — partner company data, logos, profiles
  • S3 presigned URL upload — existing file upload pattern
  • AWS Cognito — organizer and partner role authentication
  • SES email — meeting invites and minutes distribution

Technical Constraints

  • Single-operator model — no multi-user real-time editing
  • No WebSocket required — standard HTTP sufficient
  • PostgreSQL for all data, Flyway for migrations
  • Extends existing dark theme (zinc-950 + blue-400)

Business Constraints

  • Bi-annual usage = zero learning curve requirement
  • Projector-first = legible at 5 meters
  • Swiss context = financial sign-off records may be referenced by auditors
