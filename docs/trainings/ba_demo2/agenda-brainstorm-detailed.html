CROSS-POLL #32

Post-Meeting Feedback Pulse

After organizer publishes minutes, each partner receives short survey (3-4 questions): "Was the meeting productive? (1-5) / Topics we should have discussed? / Suggestions for next meeting?" Results visible to organizers, aggregated over time to spot trends.

Novelty: The meeting improves itself. Over years: "Satisfaction went from 3.2 to 4.5 since we introduced active agendas."

CROSS-POLL #33

Manual Minutes Publication & Distribution

After meeting, organizer reviews auto-assembled minutes, edits if needed, clicks "Publish & Send." All invited participants (partners, auditor, stand-ins if email provided) receive email with minutes PDF or portal link.

Novelty: Organizer stays in control — heavy lifting done by system.

CROSS-POLL #34

Action Items with Owner Assignment

At any agenda item, organizer adds action items: title, description, assigned owner (dropdown of organizers + partners), optional deadline. Distinct section in minutes.

Novelty: Decisions don't evaporate. Every action has a name next to it.

CROSS-POLL #35

Action Item Carry-Forward

When creating next partner meeting's agenda, system surfaces open action items from previous meetings as a "Review Open Actions" section. Each reviewed — mark done, update, or carry forward.

Novelty: Nothing falls through the cracks between meetings. The system remembers what humans forget.

Key Takeaways for Business Analysts

What to remember when brainstorming with the BMAD Analyst Agent

🎯 Give Rich Context

Describe what exists today in detail — entities, roles, pages, workflows. The richer the input, the more targeted the ideas.

✂️ Steer and Reject Freely

You're the domain expert. Say "none of these" to an entire batch. The agent adapts immediately.

🔍 Watch for Cross-Cutting Patterns

Meeting minutes as a living document emerged from a casual remark. Cross-cutting artifacts hide in individual feature discussions.

🎭 Role Playing Finds Edge Cases

Stand-in delegation, absent partner voting — came from being those stakeholders, not from feature brainstorming.

🚧 Constraints Are Design Inputs

"We create the report on meeting day" killed 3 features — and shaped the system toward what's actually usable.

📊 Trust AI Technique Selection

Morphological Analysis (grid), Role Playing (perspectives), Cross-Pollination (external patterns) — each built on the last.
