Session Overview

Topic: Active Partner Meeting Agenda — transforming 6 static agenda items into interactive, system-supported features within the BATbern event management platform.

Goals:

  • Make each of the 6 agenda items system-supported (not just text in an ICS invite)
  • Identify new functionality that enhances each agenda point
  • Explore how partner engagement before, during, and after the meeting can be improved

Current Agenda Items (Static)

  1. Greet all partners present (partners = users with role PARTNER)
  2. Analytics on past years events, attendances, and attendee feedback
  3. Show costs of past years + auditor's revision PDF + get sign-off for books
  4. Show last year's costs + present year's budget + new fee per partner + get sign-off
  5. Discuss current year's topics (topic voting + blob game for topic selection)
  6. Discuss other current hot architecture topics partners are interested in

Existing Platform Capabilities

  • Partner roles & auth (Epic 8 complete)
  • Attendance analytics + XLSX export
  • Topic voting system
  • ICS meeting invites
  • Partner portal with login

Technique Selection

Approach: AI-Recommended Techniques

  • Morphological Analysis: Systematically map 6 agenda items × interaction dimensions
  • Role Playing: Walk through from Partner, Organizer, Auditor, and Absent Partner perspectives
  • Cross-Pollination: Borrow proven patterns from conference platforms, workshop tools, and retrospective tools

Technique Execution Results

Phase 1: Morphological Analysis

Matrix: 6 agenda items × 5 interaction dimensions (PRE / LIVE / POST / ARTIFACT / NOTIFICATION)

A1 — Partner Greeting / Attendance

A1-PRE #1

RSVP Attendance Dashboard

Partners confirm attendance via the portal ahead of the meeting. Organizers see a live RSVP dashboard showing confirmed / declined / pending, with a headcount forecast.

Novelty: Turns passive "who showed up" into proactive planning.

A1-PRE #2

Partner Profile Cards

Each confirmed partner gets a visible profile card (photo, company, role, years as partner). Especially valuable when new partners join.

Novelty: Removes the awkward "who are you?" moment.

A1-LIVE #3

Who Is Who — Partner Gallery View

Clicking the "Greeting" agenda item opens a gallery grid of all partner companies, each showing logo, delegate name, photo, and role. Present partners get a green "Present" badge; absent ones greyed.

Novelty: Zero ceremony — no check-in ritual, no roll call.

CROSS-CUTTING #4

Auto-Generated Meeting Minutes

The system maintains a PartnerMeetingMinutes entity that builds itself as the organizer walks through the active agenda. Each agenda item contributes its section. Exportable as PDF.

Novelty: Eliminates "someone takes notes." The system already knows what was shown and decided.

A1-POST #5

Attendance Section in Minutes

Who Is Who presence data auto-populates the "Attendees" section of the meeting minutes.

Novelty: First section of minutes writes itself.

A2 — Analytics Review

A2-PRE #6

Analytics Deep-Link in Meeting Invite

The ICS invite includes a direct link to the analytics page. Zero development cost.

Novelty: Turns the invite into a preparation tool.

A2-LIVE #7

Analytics Snapshot for Minutes

System captures a snapshot of current analytics data and attaches it to the meeting minutes as an immutable historical record.

Novelty: Analytics page evolves, but minutes always reflect what was presented that day.

A2-POST #8

Historized Analytics Archive

Each partner meeting creates a dated analytics snapshot. Builds a browsable year-over-year archive.

Novelty: Routine presentations become institutional memory.

A3 — Financial Audit Sign-off

A3-LIVE #9

Audit Report Upload & Viewer

Organizer uploads the auditor's revision PDF. Embedded PDF viewer opens on click. Permanently attached to meeting record.

Novelty: No emailing PDFs around. Document lives in the system.

A3-LIVE #10

Simple Approve/Decline Vote

Organizer triggers a vote. Each present partner: Approve / Decline. System tallies and logs result.

Novelty: Replaces verbal nod with traceable record. One click per partner.

A3-POST #11

Audit Section in Meeting Minutes

Minutes get: uploaded PDF as attachment, vote result, discussion notes. PDF historized permanently.

Novelty: Complete audit trail years later.

A4 — Budget & Fee Approval

A4-LIVE #12

Budget Spreadsheet Upload & Viewer

Same upload/viewer pattern as audit PDF. Consistent UX.

Novelty: Reuses the same component.

A4-LIVE #13

Live Vote on Budget Direction

Simple approve/decline vote for budget direction and proposed new fee. Captures room sentiment.

Novelty: Softer vote — directional agreement, not binding.

A4-POST #14

Async Fee Acceptance in Partner Portal

After meeting, each partner reviews and confirms new fee in portal. Deadline reminder if no response. 100% coverage.

Novelty: Meeting creates the decision, portal operationalizes it.

A4-POST #15

Fee Acceptance Tracker

Organizer dashboard: accepted / pending / declined. Feeds into invoicing pipeline.

Novelty: Closes the loop between meeting decision and operational execution.

A5 — Topic Discussion & Voting

A5-PRE #16

Topic Voting Deep-Link in Meeting Invite

ICS invite includes direct link to topic selection/voting page. Zero new development.

Novelty: Same deep-link pattern.

A5-POST #17

Topic Discussion Results in Minutes

Minutes capture which topics discussed, blob game results, decisions and action items per topic.

Novelty: Blob game results historized.

A6 — Hot Architecture Topics

A6 #18

Open Discussion — Future Enhancement

Left as free-form. Organizer adds free-text notes to minutes.

Novelty: Intentionally unstructured.

Phase 2: Role Playing

Perspective: The New Partner

ROLE-NEW #19

New Partner Onboarding Tour

First-login guided tour of the partner platform: analytics, topic voting, partner directory, meeting agenda.

Novelty: Turns overwhelming first login into confident "I know what's going on."

ROLE-NEW #20

New Partner Context Package

System surfaces: current fee, last year's analytics, organizers, other partner companies. Auto-assembled briefing.

Novelty: No organizer prepares a welcome packet manually.

ROLE-NEW #21

"New Partner" Badge in Who Is Who

New partners (first year) get a subtle "New" badge. Signals to veterans to be welcoming.

Novelty: Tiny UI touch, huge social impact.

Perspective: The Absent Partner

ROLE-ABSENT #22

Post-Meeting Minutes Access

All partners notified when minutes published. Absent partners review everything at their own pace.

Novelty: No partner ever out of the loop.

ROLE-ABSENT #23

Async Fee Vote for Absent Partners

Same fee acceptance flow, without being in the room.

Novelty: Decouples fee decision from physical presence.

ROLE-ABSENT #24

Stand-In Delegation Record

Organizer records: "Company Y represented by [name], delegated by [partner]." No system login needed. "Represented by" badge in Who Is Who.

Novelty: Handles non-system-users. Governance trail preserved.

ROLE-ABSENT #25

Stand-In Vote Attribution

Organizer casts votes on behalf. Minutes log clear attribution chain.

Novelty: Clear proxy voting trail.

Perspective: The Organizer

ROLE-ORG #26

Agenda Builder — Select Active Items

Toggle active item types, reorder via drag-and-drop. Every meeting tailored.

Novelty: Annual meeting has all items; mid-year check-in might only have Analytics + Topics.

ROLE-ORG #27

Custom Passive Agenda Items

Free-form items (title + description) as discussion placeholders.

Novelty: Agenda never limited to what the system knows about.

ROLE-ORG #28

Meeting Prep Checklist

Auto-generated checklist based on enabled items: uploads needed, RSVP count, deadlines.

Novelty: System knows what each item needs.

ROLE-ORG #29

Agenda Controller / Presenter Mode

Agenda sidebar, current item highlighted, each shows its interaction. Quick note button always visible.

Novelty: Meeting driven like a presentation, each "slide" is interactive.

ROLE-ORG #30

Per-Item Note Taking

Free-text notes at any agenda item, contextualized in minutes under the right heading.

Novelty: Notes always contextualized.

Perspective: The Auditor

ROLE-AUDITOR #31

Auditor Email Configuration

Admin field for auditor email. Conditional auto-invite when audit item on agenda. PDF via organizer upload.

Novelty: Zero system overhead. One config field.

Phase 3: Cross-Pollination

CROSS-POLL #32

Post-Meeting Feedback Pulse

Short survey after minutes published: productivity rating, missed topics, suggestions. Aggregated over time.

Novelty: The meeting improves itself. Track satisfaction trends.

CROSS-POLL #33

Manual Minutes Publication & Distribution

Organizer reviews, edits, clicks "Publish & Send." All participants receive email with minutes.

Novelty: Organizer controls; system does heavy lifting.

CROSS-POLL #34

Action Items with Owner Assignment

Title, description, assigned owner, optional deadline. Distinct section in minutes.

Novelty: Decisions don't evaporate. Every action has a name.

CROSS-POLL #35

Action Item Carry-Forward

Open items from previous meetings auto-surface when creating next meeting's agenda.

Novelty: Nothing falls through the cracks. System remembers.

Key Architectural Decisions

  1. PartnerMeeting entity — agenda definition (active + passive items, ordered), linked documents, vote results, minutes
  2. Reusable Vote component — approve/decline for audit, budget, and future use
  3. Reusable Document Viewer — upload + embedded view for PDF and spreadsheet
  4. Meeting Minutes — assembled progressively from agenda item artifacts, editable, manually published
  5. Action Item entity — title, owner, deadline, status, linked to meeting + agenda item, carry-forward logic
